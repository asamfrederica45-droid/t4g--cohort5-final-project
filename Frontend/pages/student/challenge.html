    01 — OBJECTIVE

    What will you learn?

    You will explore how different materials can be used to remove visible impurities from water.

    02 — MATERIALS

    What do you need?

    • ✓ A plastic bottle
    • ✓ Clean sand
    • ✓ Small stones or gravel
    • ✓ Cotton or cloth
    • ✓ A container
    • ✓ Dirty water for testing

    03 — INSTRUCTIONS

    Let's do it!

    1

    Cut the bottom of the plastic bottle carefully.

    2

    Place cotton or cloth near the bottle opening.

    3

    Add layers of sand and gravel.

    4

    Pour the dirty water through the filter and observe what happens.

    REAL-LIFE CONNECTION

    Where can you see this concept?

    Filtration is used in many water treatment systems. Think about how the materials you used in this activity helped remove visible particles from the water.
